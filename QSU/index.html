Mission

Qatar Student Union aims to turn student hobbies and interests into meaningful pursuits by taking initiative and creating sub-branches for each individual interest, whether it's journalism, performance arts, sports, or anything else students want to build a community around. Each branch gives students a space to actually develop that interest, through practice, projects, volunteering and connecting with others who share the same passions, rather than leaving it as just a passing hobby. Additionally, we aim to counsel students on any inquiries regarding extracurricular activities, whilst also providing volunteering opportunities.

Vision

Qatar Student Union envisions a future where every student has a branch that speaks to who they are, a community that shares their passions, and assistance and opportunities to grow beyond the classroom and into the real world whilst also having access to counselling guidance!
